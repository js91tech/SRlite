SRlite / Roadside Radar

Capabilities Breakdown — What This App Will Accomplish When Complete

Prepared for js91tech · Market: Marietta, GA (25-mile radius) · GitHub: github.com/js91tech/SRlite · June 2026

Executive summary: SRlite is a real-time dispatch lead-fishing command center for a roadside/tow company that owns its own trucks. It aggregates opportunities from multiple sources, scores them by likelihood of conversion, shows them on a live map alongside your fleet, and lets dispatchers assign the nearest truck in one click — so you find profitable jobs before competitors do.

1. Business Problem It Solves

Roadside assistance companies compete on speed and visibility. Leads arrive from many channels — marketplace apps (Honk, Urgently), traffic incidents, social media posts, police rotation lists, inbound calls, and branded help links — often with no single view of what is happening in your territory right now.

SRlite centralizes those signals into one dispatcher dashboard optimized for finding leads (fishing) rather than only managing jobs already won. For a Marietta-based operator covering I-75, I-285, and I-575 corridors, it answers: Where is the next good call, how hot is it, and which truck can get there fastest?

2.3 Intelligent Lead Scoring

Every lead receives a composite score (0–100+) so dispatchers work the best opportunities first:

  • Source weight — marketplace offers and self-reports rank above passive incident feeds
  • Freshness bonus — newer leads score higher; stale leads decay
  • Contactable bonus — leads with phone/SMS contact get +15 points
  • Corridor bonus — incidents on hot interstates get extra points
  • Truck proximity bonus — nearest available unit within 15–25 min ETA boosts score

Score breakdown is stored per lead for transparency. High-score alerts trigger sound notifications in the dashboard.

2.4 Dispatcher Command Center

  • Live map — Leaflet map with territory circle, lead pins (color-coded by score), and fleet truck markers
  • Alert tray — sorted lead list with source labels, time ago, roadway, and nearest truck ETA
  • Fish mode — open opportunities to pursue (fishing for new business)
  • Jobs mode — assigned/active work in progress
  • Contactable filter — show only leads you can call or text immediately
  • Lead detail panel — full issue description, contact info, score breakdown, quote templates
  • One-click assign — assign nearest available truck and create a job record
  • Quick stage — fast-assign using pre-calculated nearest truck
  • Dismiss — remove low-value or expired leads from the active tray

2.5 Fleet Management

  • Track owned trucks: label, type, capabilities, status (available / busy / offline)
  • Real-time position on map (demo: 3 units; future: GPS integration)
  • Automatic nearest-truck calculation with distance and ETA
  • Job creation on assignment with quote amount and customer phone

2.6 Driver & Customer Touchpoints

  • /help page — public branded form for stranded motorists (phone + issue + browser GPS)
  • Dashboard “+ Report” button — dispatcher can open help link to simulate/test intake
  • Quote templates — pre-built pricing/SMS templates per service type (tow, jump, lockout, etc.)
  • Future: driver mobile app — accept jobs, navigate, update status from the field

2.7 Real-Time Operations

  • WebSocket push alerts — new leads appear instantly without page refresh
  • Audio alert on high-priority incoming leads
  • 511GA background poller — continuous scan for new traffic incidents in territory
  • Lead clustering and deduplication via external source IDs
  • Auto-expire stale leads after configurable timeout (default 20 min)

5. Day-in-the-Life Workflow (Complete)

  1. Morning: Dispatcher opens SRlite dashboard. Territory circle shows Marietta + 25 mi. Three trucks appear as green (available) on the map.
  2. Incident arrives: 511GA poller detects an accident on I-75 northbound inside territory. Lead appears in Alert Tray with score ~65. Map pin drops. Optional sound plays.
  3. Marketplace offer: Honk pushes a $95 tow offer near I-285. Score jumps to 92 (high source weight + corridor + nearby truck). Dispatcher clicks lead, reviews payout and ETA.
  4. Self-report: A motorist submits the branded /help form from their phone. Contactable lead with phone number scores 95+. Dispatcher calls customer, selects quote template, assigns Truck 2.
  5. Social signal: Reddit listener flags “stranded on I-575” post. Lead is contactable via DM link. Dispatcher stages nearest truck.
  6. Job mode: Assigned leads move to Jobs tab. Dispatcher tracks active work while continuing to fish for new opportunities.
  7. End of shift: Dismissed/expired leads clear automatically. Job history retained in database for reporting.

6. Success Metrics

  • Time to first contact — seconds from lead creation to dispatcher action
  • Lead conversion rate — % of fished leads that become paid jobs
  • Revenue per truck-hour — marketplace payout + direct job revenue
  • Geographic coverage — % of territory incidents captured vs. missed
  • Source ROI — which channels (Honk, 511, social, self-report) produce the best jobs
